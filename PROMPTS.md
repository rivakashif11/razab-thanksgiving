# Razab USA — Thanksgiving 2026 Creative & Art Direction Guide
## Clean, Modern, Airy & Botanical Image Generation Prompts (Midjourney v6.1 & DALL-E 3)

This creative guide has been updated to reflect Razab’s **clean, modern, airy, and aesthetic Thanksgiving aesthetic**. All heavy dark grunge, drips, and gothic shadows have been replaced with **soft morning window light, fresh culinary herbs, bright neutral linen, and a refined botanical palette**:

- **Sage Leaf:** `#C3D692`
- **Forest Sage:** `#64875D`
- **Spiced Chestnut:** `#8F544A`
- **Terracotta Copper:** `#C87F4E`
- **Warm Honey Gold:** `#F7CC83`
- **Base Canvas:** Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) and Crisp Studio White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

### Core Art Direction Principles
1. **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Bright natural diffused morning/afternoon daylight streaming through clean windows. No heavy vignette, no moody dark shadows, no gothic candlelight.
2. **Tabletop & Materials:** Clean light bleached oak or warm cream linen tablecloths, fresh sage sprigs, small white heirloom gourds, delicate copper utensils, and pristine crystal-clear borosilicate glassware.
3. **Framing:** Clean modern rectangular photography with smooth `8px–10px` rounded corners, blending seamlessly with `#FAF7F2` and `#FFFFFF` cards.
